## 2. Binary Number Representation

### A. Decimal 75 to Binary

Repeated division by 2:

75 ÷ 2 = 37 remainder 1  
37 ÷ 2 = 18 remainder 1  
18 ÷ 2 = 9 remainder 0  
9 ÷ 2 = 4 remainder 1  
4 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 0  
2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0  
1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1  

Reading remainders from bottom to top:

**75 = 1001011 (Binary)**

### B. Binary 110101 to Decimal

Using positional weights:

= 1×2⁵ + 1×2⁴ + 0×2³ + 1×2² + 0×2¹ + 1×2⁰

= 32 + 16 + 0 + 4 + 0 + 1

**= 53 (Decimal)**

### C. Why Computers Use Binary

Computers use binary because electronic circuits can easily represent two states, ON (1) and OFF (0), making data processing reliable and efficient.
